# Code of Conduct

Splurter is a small open-source project. We want it to be a friendly, safe place to ask questions, report problems and send changes, whatever your experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Critique the code, not the person. Give specific, useful feedback and accept it graciously.
- Respect privacy. Don't share other people's personal information, and don't post screenshots with private content (notifications, messages, contacts, mirrored screens).
- Stay on topic and keep discussion constructive.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexual or violent language, doxxing, spam, and anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, message the maintainer, [@anaslari23](https://github.com/anaslari23), privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are treated confidentially. For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
